    end

2 · Subscription Lifecycle (state machine)

stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> trialing: start 1-month trial (card captured)
    trialing --> active: trial-end charge succeeds
    trialing --> canceled: user cancels (access until trial_end)
    trialing --> past_due: trial-end charge fails
    active --> past_due: renewal charge fails
    active --> canceled: user cancels (access until period_end)
    past_due --> active: retry succeeds / card updated
    past_due --> canceled: dunning exhausted
    canceled --> active: resubscribe
    canceled --> expired: period_end passes
    expired --> active: resubscribe

flowchart TD

Principles

Language-first, always

The learner's language is chosen once and respected everywhere — UI, glosses, receipts, reminders. Thai and Vietnamese strings get equal typographic care (Noto Sans Thai fallback is loaded).

Professional, not childish

Navy + cinnabar with restrained gamification (streaks, XP, progress rings) keeps credibility for HSK candidates while staying warm for casual learners.

Hanzi as the hero

Characters render large in Noto Serif SC; pinyin is always the red accent line beneath. This pairing is consistent from marketing page to lesson player.

Honest billing UX

The 1-month trial is presented with explicit charge dates, reminder promises, and one-click cancel — matching the dunning and trial flows in the architecture doc.
